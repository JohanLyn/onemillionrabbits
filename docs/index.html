HOW TO PLAY

MoveWASD / arrow keys. On a phone, drag anywhere.
AttackYour weapons fire on their own. Focus on not being smothered.
Level upGrab carrots to level up and pick new nonsense. Up to 6 weapons and 6 trinkets.
FuseMax out two compatible weapons and they fuse into something even dumber.
BreedingRabbits left alone near each other make babies. The counter goes up. Kill fast.
Wheel of ChaosEvery wave spins a wheel that bends the rules for that wave.
Orbital Carrot StrikeDefeat rabbits to charge it, then press SPACE or tap the carrot button.
BossesEvery 5th wave a royal rabbit shows up. It drops a treasure chest.
